## Remaining platform boundaries

- Android screen-sharing consent cannot be silently restored after revocation/restart/update. The launcher can preserve a still image, not grant itself capture access.
- Protected video may be black. Preview detection is conservative; supporting a format does not guarantee every reader exposes an identifiable page surface.
- Resume reopens a retained third-party task; Snapplication cannot own that application's playback buffer or guarantee instant network/player readiness.
- Previous/next require media controls exposed by the player. Loading an unknown next episode in arbitrary apps is not universally available.
- HDMI mirror/desktop selection belongs to the device projection UI. Hardware Power may never reach Accessibility, and wake support depends on the receiver/device.
- Keep online streaming checks under 10 seconds, including Family Guy. Prefer the existing local VLC QA video.

## 6 October continuation — touch recorder and phone results

`TouchMacroRecorder` now provides an explicit, one-finger overlay recorder. It forwards each gesture once after the finger lifts, suppresses accessibility echoes, stores a bounded normalized path and separate wait/duration, and removes the overlay on finish, error or timeout. Maximum gesture is three seconds; multi-touch and text/password/keyboard screens are excluded. Replay requires the same app, orientation, window dimensions and structural layout. Unexpected context stops playback rather than retrying an uncertain touch. Existing semantic button recording remains available by disabling the taps/swipes checkbox. The remote Record/stop action uses the last chosen recording mode.

Android on the Mate 10 Pro initially retained capabilities=9 after update. The user explicitly turned the support switches off/on; Universal then reported capabilities=41, including gesture dispatch. A new preflight check prevents a misleading partial recording when Android still has the older capability set. Edition-specific accessibility names now distinguish Universal, Reader and TV.
