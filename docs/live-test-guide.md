# Your live test build

Start with **snapplication-universal-debug.apk** from `private-test-builds`. Transfer it to your phone and open it to install. It updates the previous Universal test build. E-reader and TV APKs are separate installations with their own settings.

## Set up your Home

1. Open Snapplication, then **All Apps**. Tap a star to pin an app; tap an empty **+** Home block to return to the app list.
2. Open **Launcher Settings** from the bottom of the app drawer. Choose your theme, enable **Transparency** if wanted, and choose your Android wallpaper.
3. Under **Read & Watch**, choose your mode and **Main viewer**:
   - **Continue app** gives you a quick return to the last app launched through Snapplication.
   - **Folder content** previews a video, PDF, comic or image you select through **Choose folder**, then **Library**.
4. Leave **Play videos in launcher** / **Read in launcher** off to open the selected content in another installed app. Turn them on to use the built-in player/reader. A compatible external app is required for external opening.
5. Use **Content sizing** for Fit, Fill or Stretch. To change pages inside the built-in reader, enable **Previous / Next buttons**.

Landscape Home fills the screen. Swipe from a screen edge to reveal Android navigation. The small **All Apps** bar opens the drawer; swiping up from the shortcut area also opens it.

Use **Set as default launcher** in Settings when you are ready to try it as your regular Home. You can change back through Android's default Home app settings.

## Feedback that helps

Use it normally. Send the edition, device/Android version, what you tapped, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot or short recording is useful for layout problems. Your own live testing will cover the phone, hub, remote and display; no further hardware setup with the assistant is required.

These are private development builds. All features are free, with no ads or paid unlocks. Other apps control their own saved position; generic file opening cannot guarantee identical resume behavior in every player or reader. Provider plugins and hardware-specific e-ink/CEC/charging controls are not included.
